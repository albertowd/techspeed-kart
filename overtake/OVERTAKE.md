[HEADING=1]techspeed-kart[/HEADING]

The Techspeed karts raced at Velopark, Brazil: the rental kart with its steel perimeter protector in three Honda engine versions, and the racing kart without the protector with a 125cc two-stroke. Modelled from photos of the real karts, for Assetto Corsa and BeamNG.drive.

[LIST]
[*][B]Rental v2:[/B] the kart from the rental sessions, with the black steel protector all around, side pods, an engine cover, full rear bumper with its reflector stickers and a governed Honda GX engine on a centrifugal clutch.
[*][B]Rental v1:[/B] the earlier rental, with a flat black protector band on rubber buffers instead of the steel tubes, no side pods and the engine in the open.
[*][B]Racing 125:[/B] the race chassis without the protector, water-cooled 125cc direct drive with the radiator on the left and brakes on all four wheels. Its engine figures are an estimate until the real ones are in.

[HEADING=2]Features[/HEADING]

[LIST]
[*]Chassis, spindles, axle, brakes, engines, seat and bodywork modelled from photos of the real karts.
[*]No driving aids, no lights, no LEDs, like the real karts.
[*]Direct drive with a centrifugal clutch, solid rear axle and the sprocket choices of the real karts.
[*]The rental fleets' real liveries, karts 1 to 36, every third one on worn tyres.
[*]BeamNG.drive: a soft-body kart with a rigid steel frame and plastic bodywork that shakes on its mounts.
[/LIST]

[HEADING=2]Installing[/HEADING]

[B]Assetto Corsa:[/B] extract the [B]techspeed-kart-<version>-ac[/B] 7z into the [B]assettocorsa[/B] folder; it adds the six [B]techspeed_rental_*[/B] cars to [B]content/cars[/B].

[B]BeamNG.drive:[/B] put the [B]techspeed-kart-<version>-beamng[/B] zip in BeamNG's [B]mods[/B] folder, then pick Techspeed Kart in the vehicle list; the six rentals are its configurations.

The karts were made for the Techspeed Velopark track, also on overtake.gg.
